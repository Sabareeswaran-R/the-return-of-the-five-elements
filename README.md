# The Return of the Five Elements

**Author:** Sabareeswaran R

## About the Story

A fantasy story about sacrifice, betrayal, motherhood, and the legacy of five elemental powers.

## Story

Sera was a woman blessed with the power to control five elements—earth, water, fire, air, and sky. Yet she never used her gifts for herself. Wherever people needed help, she went, regardless of kingdom or status.

While carrying her unborn child, Sera was deceived by King Bersen. He promised that her powers were needed to protect his kingdom from its enemies. Trusting him, Sera entered the battlefield and defeated the opposing army using the five elements.

But Bersen feared her.

He thought that the power which protected his kingdom today might threaten his throne tomorrow. So, after the battle, he betrayed Sera and killed her. Her body was thrown from a mountain, and Bersen believed her power had died with her.

He was wrong.

Mountain people found Sera and discovered that her unborn child was still alive. They rescued him. On the day the child was born, the earth trembled, storms roared, the sea rose, rain poured, and a volcano erupted. They believed Sera's five powers had passed into her son.

They named him **Ares**, Sera's name reversed.

The mountain queen, **Reena**, who had no children of her own, raised Ares as her son and taught him that power existed to protect, not destroy.

Years later, Bersen invaded their land. When he hurt Reena, Ares confronted him. An old woman finally revealed the truth: Reena was his mother by love, Sera by blood—and Bersen had murdered Sera.

Ares unleashed all five elements and defeated Bersen.

But he did not inherit Bersen's cruelty.

The people crowned Ares as their new king.

He ruled with Sera's power and Reena's love, turning the five elements from weapons of war into guardians of peace.

> One mother gave him life.  
> Another gave him love.  
> Together, their voices made a king.
